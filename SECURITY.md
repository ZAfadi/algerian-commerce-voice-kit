# Security and privacy

- Keep Gemini and transcription credentials server-side.
- Supply only the minimum data required to speak the approved message.
- Do not commit customer phone numbers, addresses, recordings, or transcripts.
- Treat generated audio as untrusted until its back-transcript passes verification.
- Keep prices, availability, consent, order state, and irreversible actions in deterministic application code.
- Review retention and consent requirements before recording or storing calls.
